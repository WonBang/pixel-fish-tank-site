없습니다. 픽셀어항은 어떠한 개인정보도 수집·저장·전송하지 않습니다. 계정이 없고, 로그인이 없으며, 분석 도구나 광고 SDK, 크래시 리포터를 포함하지 않습니다.

앱은 어떤 서버와도 통신하지 않습니다. 모든 화면과 이미지는 앱 안에 포함되어 있으며, 오프라인에서 동일하게 동작합니다.

Privacy Policy

Pixel Aquarium (Cyber Fish Tank) · Last updated September 3, 2026

Information we collect

None. Pixel Aquarium collects, stores, and transmits no personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs of any kind.

What is stored on your device

Your progress — fish, currency, encyclopedia, achievements — is saved in the app's own browser storage (localStorage) on your device only. It never leaves the device, nobody (the developer included) can access it, and deleting the app deletes it.

Network activity

The app contacts no servers. Every screen and image ships inside the app, and it behaves identically offline.

Sharing with third parties

We collect nothing, so there is nothing to share or sell.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, regardless of age.

If this policy changes, the updated text and revision date will be posted here.
